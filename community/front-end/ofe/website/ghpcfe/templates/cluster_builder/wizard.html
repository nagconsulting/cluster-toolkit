Create a cluster Gemini preview

Gemini ranks the Cluster Toolkit blueprints in examples/ and community/examples/ for your workload. You then configure the one you pick, with Gemini's help; every change is validated with gcluster expand. Nothing is deployed.

Becomes the blueprint's deployment_name.
By default the blueprint creates its own network, created and destroyed with the cluster. Choose a subnet of an OFE network to put the cluster on it instead: Gemini swaps the blueprint's network for it and keeps any GPU networks.
